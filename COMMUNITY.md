# Aiden Community

Aiden Community is the open-source, local-first edition of Aiden. The current Community feature generation is v4.20 and remains available under the existing AGPL-3.0 license.

Community maintenance continues through v4.20.x releases for critical bugs, security and compatibility fixes, runtime correctness, and core trust and safety improvements.

The Community edition includes the local runtime, durable Jobs and Attempts, Workbench, Browser Operator, Apps and External Coding foundations, Live Execution, approvals, Evidence, Verification, Proof, recovery, cancellation, and artifacts.

Commercial offerings may add managed workflows, automation, remote access, collaboration, hosted services, maintained premium capabilities, and commercial support. They do not revoke or reduce the rights already granted for Aiden Community, and core safety and truthfulness protections remain part of Community maintenance.

Any future decision to publish a new Community feature generation will be made explicitly. Until then, public Community feature development remains on v4.20 with v4.20.x maintenance releases.
